# Privacy Policy

## Overview

MirageForge is committed to protecting participant privacy. This document explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## What We Collect

### Anonymous Data Only
- Session UUID (random identifier)
- Stimulus responses (choice, reaction time)
- Optional confidence ratings
- Timestamps (for analysis)

### What We DON'T Collect
- No IP addresses
- No user agents or browser information
- No names or personal identifiers
- No email addresses
- No phone numbers
- No physical addresses
- No demographic information
- No location data
- No camera or microphone access
- No cookies beyond session management

## How We Use Data

### Research Purposes
- Analyze visual perception patterns
- Compare human and model responses
- Identify candidate stimuli for validation
- Generate aggregate statistics

### Data Sharing
- Only aggregate, anonymized data shared
- No individual response data shared publicly
- Data shared under appropriate research license
- Compliance with data protection regulations

## Data Protection

### Storage
- Local database (SQLite/PostgreSQL)
- No cloud storage or external services
- No third-party analytics
- Encrypted at rest (optional)

### Security
- Security headers (CSP, X-Content-Type-Options)
- CSRF protection
- Rate limiting
- Admin token authentication

### Retention
- Anonymous data retained for research purposes
- Cannot delete individual responses (no identifiers)
- Participants informed of this limitation in consent
- Entire studies can be deleted by administrators

## Your Rights

### Before Participation
- Right to be informed about data collection
- Right to ask questions
- Right to decline participation

### During Participation
- Right to stop at any time
- Right to skip questions
- Right to withdraw without penalty

### After Participation
- Right to contact researchers with questions
- **Limitation**: Cannot delete individual anonymous responses
- Right to request deletion of entire study (admin only)

## Contact

For questions about privacy or data protection:
- Check documentation in docs/ directory
- Contact research team (see study-specific consent)
- Report issues via GitHub repository

## Changes to Privacy Policy

- Privacy policy version tracked with consent
- Participants informed of changes
- New consent required for material changes

## Compliance

### Data Protection Regulations
- Designed with GDPR principles
- Privacy by design approach
- Minimal data collection
- No cross-border data transfer

### Institutional Requirements
- Complies with standard research ethics requirements
- Supports IRB/ethics committee oversight
- Transparent data practices
